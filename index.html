    About the municipality

    Doña Remedios Trinidad

    Doña Remedios Trinidad (DRT) was established as a municipality in 1977 through Presidential Decree No. 1196. It was formed from parts of Angat, Norzagaray, and San Miguel, Bulacan, and was named after Doña Remedios Trinidad.

    Today, DRT is known as Bulacan’s largest municipality by land area, home to vast forests, mountains, rivers, and rural communities.
